West Visayas State University Panayana Cultural Group

Preserving, promoting, and staging the rich cultural heritage of Western Visayas and the Philippines through world-class folk dancing, authentic indigenous instrumentation, and theatrical repertoires.

Our History & Roots

Founded at West Visayas State University in Iloilo City, the Panayana Cultural Group has served as a beacon of artistic excellence and Philippine folkloric heritage.

Mission & Vision

To cultivate disciplined student performers who embody the cultural soul of Ilonggo tradition and Philippine heritage.

Former Advisers & Artistic Directors

Honoring the visionaries and faculty mentors who shaped the Panayana legacy through decades of artistic leadership.
